# SEO keyword plan: Fathima Driving School

I chose keywords by search intent and by what you can honestly claim. I do not have access to live search-volume data here, so check the final list in Google Keyword Planner and Google Search Console after a few weeks.

## Keywords used (and where)
| Keyword | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Driving school in Srivilliputtur** | Home: title, H1, intro, headings |
| **Fathima Driving School Srivilliputtur** | Home intro, footer, About, image alt text (brand search) |
| **Driving school near Aathukkadai Street, Srivilliputtur** | Contact H1, Home local section |
| **Driving institute in Srivilliputtur, Virudhunagar district** | Home local section heading, About page |
| **Driving school near me** | Used as "a driving school near you" in the Home intro (Google decides "near me" from the searcher's location, so your Google Business Profile matters most) |
| Car driving classes in Srivilliputtur | Four-wheeler page: URL, title, H1 |
| Two-wheeler riding classes in Srivilliputtur | Two-wheeler page: URL, title, H1 |
| Motor driving school | About page, once, naturally |
| Learner's licence / driving licence, Srivilliputtur | Licence guide page |

## Keywords I did NOT use, and why
- **Best / Top / Trusted driving school**: these are claims. Use them only when you can back them (for example with real Google reviews). Ask happy students to review you on Google, then these words come naturally.
- **Government approved driving school**: add this only if it is true. There is a marked spot in `about.html` (search for "government/RTO approved") to add one honest sentence.
- **Driving classes near Srivilliputtur bus stand**: add only if you are actually close to the bus stand. Do not guess a distance.

## Technical SEO included on every page
Unique title and meta description, one H1, canonical URL, Open Graph tags, LocalBusiness + Breadcrumb + FAQ structured data (JSON-LD), consistent name/address/phone, descriptive image alt text, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, internal links between pages, mobile-friendly layout.

## What to do after going live (this matters more than any keyword)
1. Replace `https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com` everywhere (Find and Replace in VS Code).
2. Create and verify your **Google Business Profile**, add the website, photos and opening hours.
3. Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
4. Ask students for Google reviews.
5. Use the same name, address and phone number everywhere online.
6. Replace the two online photos with real photos of your cars, bikes and students.
