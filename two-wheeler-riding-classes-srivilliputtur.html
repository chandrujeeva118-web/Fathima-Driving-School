Two-wheeler riding classes in Srivilliputtur. Balance first, speed later.

What you will practise, in order

  1. Balance and posture

    Walking the bike, standing balance and a relaxed grip.

  2. Clutch, gears and brakes

    Where each control is, and how much pressure it needs.

  3. Slow riding and the figure-of-eight

    The control skill the licence test looks for.

  4. Road riding

    Turns, overtaking safely, and reading traffic around you.

  5. Helmet and safety habits

    Fit, visibility and the checks to make before every ride.

  6. Mock licence test

    A practice run of the test track and road routine.

Gearless two-wheelers up to 50cc: 16 with guardian consent. Geared motorcycles: 18. Please confirm current rules with the RTO.
