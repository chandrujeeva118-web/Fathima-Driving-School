What you will practise, in order

  1. Cabin check

    Seat, mirrors, pedals, gears and dashboard, before the engine starts.

  2. Clutch and moving off

    Smooth starts, stops and gear changes on a quiet stretch.

  3. Steering and road sense

    Lane position, turns, signals and keeping a safe gap.

  4. Junctions and traffic

    Roundabouts, busy stretches, and sharing the road with buses and bikes.

  5. Reverse and parking

    Reversing, parallel and angle parking: the part most learners dread.

  6. Mock licence test

    A full practice of what the examiner will ask you to do.

The minimum age for a car licence is 18. Rules can change, so confirm with the RTO before you apply.

Before you book

Can I start car driving classes with no experience?

Yes. Most learners begin from zero. We start in the stationary car, then move to a quiet road.

Do I need a learner’s licence first?

You need one to practise on public roads. Tell us at enquiry and we will explain the steps.

How many days will it take?

It depends on how quickly you settle in. After your first session we suggest a plan.
