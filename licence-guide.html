Licence guide, Srivilliputtur

From learner’s licence to driving test, in order.

  1. Apply for a learner’s licence

    Applications are made through the government’s Parivahan Sarathi portal. You will need proof of age, proof of address, photographs and the fee. The exact documents can change, so check the portal first.

  2. Pass the learner’s test

    This is a test of traffic rules and road signs. Study the signs and rules before you book your slot.

  3. Practise with a trained instructor

    With a learner’s licence you can practise on public roads under the conditions the rules set. This is where our lessons begin.

  4. Book your driving test

    A permanent licence test is generally available after the learner’s licence has been held for 30 days. Confirm the current rule on the portal or with the RTO.

  5. Take the driving test

    You will be asked to show the skills for your vehicle. We run a mock test before this so nothing feels new.

This is a general guide, not legal advice. Fees, documents and waiting periods change, so verify them on the official portal before you apply.
