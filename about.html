About us

About Fathima Driving School, Srivilliputtur.

Fathima Driving School is a motor driving school and driving institute in Srivilliputtur, Virudhunagar district, on Aathukkadai Street. We teach cars and two-wheelers to first-time learners and to people who want more confidence on the road.

What your first session looks like

  1. Talk first

    Tell us what you want to drive and how comfortable you feel. There are no silly questions.

  2. Check the vehicle

    Mirrors, seat or stance, controls and brakes, before anything moves.

  3. Practise somewhere quiet

    Starting, stopping and steering where traffic cannot rush you.

  4. Agree the next sessions

    You leave knowing what we will practise next and when.

What we teach

Car driving classes cover clutch control, junctions, reversing and parking.

Two-wheeler riding classes cover balance, gears, braking and the figure-of-eight.
